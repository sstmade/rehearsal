## Tier A (ground truth)

- **HOPE-Image** (val scenes 6, 7, 8): © 2020 NVIDIA Corporation, [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Tyree et al., *6-DoF Pose Estimation of Household Objects for Robotic Manipulation: An Accessible Dataset and Benchmark*, IROS 2022. Obtained via the BOP benchmark ([huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/hope](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/hope)).
- **YCB-Video** (BOP'19 test frames from scenes 48, 50, 52, 55, 57, 59): © 2017 Robotics and State Estimation Lab, University of Washington, MIT License. Xiang et al., *PoseCNN*, RSS 2018; Hodaň et al., BOP benchmark ([huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/ycbv](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/ycbv)).
- Table-plane fits, table-frame coordinates and homographies in the JSON files are derived by the Rehearsal team.
