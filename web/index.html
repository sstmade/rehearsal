Sim-only rehearsal for home robots · hackathon build

Models propose,
physics decides.

A phone photo of your desk becomes a MuJoCo physics twin with a Franka Panda arm. Meta Muse Spark and SAM 3.1 propose the objects, the goals and the plan — then physics executes, logs every contact, and the model reads the log to fix its own mistake before any real robot moves.

Watch the rehearsal Try a photo
    01

    Photo → twin

    One photo. Muse Spark names each object and guesses size, mass and friction; SAM 3.1 segments it; a homography puts it on the table. Anything the models were unsure about is flagged — that doubt is carried all the way into the stress test.

    Drop a phone photo or click to choose · an A4 sheet on the table gives scale

    02

    Instruction → goals → plan

    Muse Spark compiles a natural-language instruction into checkable predicates and a plan of pick-and-place actions. Implicit goals ("don't disturb anything else") are added by the model — the simulator will hold it to every one of them.

        03

        Rehearse

        Physics runs the plan. When a goal fails, the model is handed the simulator's evidence — contacts with timestamps, IK errors, failed predicates — and must explain and repair the plan. No retries without a reason.

        04

        Parallel universes

        One success proves little. The final plan is replayed in 40 randomised universes — friction ×0.6–1.4, mass ×0.7–1.3, and the perception error the photo pipeline is actually expected to have.

        05

        Look again 再看一眼

        Active perception: which single object is worth a second photo? Re-observing one object shrinks its perception error from σ=1.2 cm to σ=0.4 cm on the same seeds — so the gain is a paired comparison, not luck.

        06

        Report

        Every claim is labelled by how it was earned. This is what gets handed to a human before a robot gets handed a mug.

        ✓Verified in simulation

        • Reachability: top-down IK to each object's top face
        • Collisions: what the arm and the held object touched, and when
        • Goal predicates: inside, on, stacked, upright, stable, undisturbed

        ≈Estimated under uncertainty

        • Friction ×0.6–1.4, mass ×0.7–1.3
        • Perception error per object, σ from the photo pipeline
        • Success rate and the parameter band where failures cluster

        !Assumed, not verified

        • Grasping is a suction weld: aligned top face ⇒ success
        • Objects are primitive shapes (box, cylinder, bowl)
        • Sim only. No real robot was moved.
